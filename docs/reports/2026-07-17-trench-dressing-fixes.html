DESPATCH — THE TRENCHES SET RIGHT

SECOND ARMY · FLANDERS · 17 JULY 1916 (2026) · re: field report “something's not quite right with the new trenches” · issue #28 · follows PR #21

THE COMPLAINT

From the line, verbatim: “Almost everything is rotated incorrectly by 90 degrees” and “there is no real step for soldiers to stand on.” Sandbag courses scattered like garden walls across No Man's Land, revetment towering over the parados like fencing, duckboards lying across the trench like ladders — and men still hovering at the junctions.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG

1 · The quarter-turn. All three props rebuilt in PR #21 — duckboardGeometry, sandbagCourseGeometry, revetmentPanelGeometry — are modelled X-long (their own comments say “local +X runs along the wall”), but Scenery.dress yawed them with atan2(dx, dz), which aligns local +Z with the trench segment. Every one of them lay at right angles to the line it was dressing.

2 · Panels seated mid-slope. The plank revetment took its base height from heightAt at the wall point — which, on a 1 m terrain grid, reads half-way up the cut wall. Tops poked ~1.5 m above the parados crest.

3 · A step that didn't read. The carved fire-step bench is real geometry (top ≈ grade − 0.45 m, verified in the heightfield) — but a 1.55 m riser across a single 1 m cell renders as a smooth ramp. Nothing said step.

4 · Junctions un-dug the bench. The communication trenches carve after the parapet lines and cut pre − depth wherever they pass — including straight through the bench at every junction, re-raising the trench mask as they went. Fighting slots there floated up to 0.89 m.

5 · Slots on the seam. fireStepSlot: 0.7 parked men on the bilinear seam between cleared-mask bench cells and full-mask floor cells — a residual ~0.3 m synthetic lift at 11 of 43 slots.

THE WORKS CARRIED OUT

Yaw from the wall normal. Every prop is now yawed with atan2(nx, nz) of its wall normal: local +X runs the line, +Z faces where it should — bags out over the parapet, revetment boards into the trench, duckboard stringers down the floor, treads across it.

Seat on the floor. Revetment panels seat at the deep-floor sample; tops now sit 0.2–0.5 m below the ground behind them (measured mid-segment).

Face the riser. A third instanced system — revetmentPanelGeometry(1.5), seated on the floor, boards toward the parados — planks the bench's riser. From the play camera the trench now reads floor → plank face → bench → sandbagged parapet: a built step.

Guard the bench. Parapet carves record their bench cells (benchGuard); the comm carve clamps its cut at the recorded bench top and leaves the mask cleared there. The junction keeps its step.

Centre the slots. fireStepSlot 0.7 → 1.0 puts fighting slots on the middle of the bench plateau (spans ~0.4–1.6 m past centreline), clear of the mask seam. standSurface itself is untouched — the cover model is numerically as tuned.
