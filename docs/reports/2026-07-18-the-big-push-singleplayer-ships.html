What you can play tonight

Title screen → The Big Push → pick your war

  • Raid (10′) · Battle (20′) · Grand Assault (35′) · Attrition — timed matches end at the whistle: enemy front sections held, tie broken by remaining battalion strength. Holding a majority of the enemy's front for a full minute ends it early, any length.
  • Three opposing commanders: Gen. von Halten keeps 2+ men on every bay and bites-and-holds next to his gains; Obst. Sturmfeld hoards requisition, then hammers one section behind a box barrage; Maj. Fuchs finds your thinnest stretch and sends a second party round the flank.
  • Men arrive, they do not appear. Every purchase despatches a column from your rear edge, up the communication trench, to its post — with an ETA, and with everything shellfire implies on the way.
  • Over the top: click your front sections (brass rings), click a German section — whistle, ladders, wire, melee, capture. Captured trenches face the wrong way until consolidated (the fire step is really re-carved — cover is terrain height, always). B recalls.
  • The camera leash: you may look across no-man's-land freely; you may not be there. The camera travels only as far as your farthest forward living soldier — advances follow in half a second, deaths ease you back over three.
The architecture underneath

One spine: AI opponent ≡ remote human ≡ replay

Every act of command — buy, assault, recall, barrage — is a tick-stamped Cmd applied only at tick boundaries, in (tick, side, seq) order. The German AI is just the first peer: a pure function of (state, persona, seeded rand) whose envelopes are never logged, because any replay or lockstep client re-derives them from the seed. The twin-sim probe runs two full sims in one process on one command stream and compares 32-bit state hashes every second; the replay probe feeds the recorded log to a third sim and demands the identical final hash. M5's multiplayer is this same machinery with a WebRTC DataChannel where the local queue is.

Field notes

What the probes taught us

  • A lone MG08 pinned a wave open for two sim-hours. The twin probe found it in its first run: a gun team dug in at extreme range, morale 1.0, forever. A stall-breaker now withdraws beaten stragglers (never men still fighting in the trench) 240 s after the last spawn.
  • Browser timer throttling can lie to you. Hidden pages clamp setTimeout to once a minute; the probes now yield via MessageChannel and run at full speed regardless.
  • The AI beats a lazy line 49–1 — the lab bot leaves its purse unspent. A human playing normally (seven posts, a Vickers, rapid fire) shot Methodical's first big push flat 22–0 and took the whistle 99:61. The economy rewards spending; balance feels right for a first ship, and the lab is standing by for tuning passes.
