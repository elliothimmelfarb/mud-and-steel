Tracers now leave the barrel

#14 · PR #20
Gunfeel — the flagged priority
Root cause, measured live: the muzzle flash was innocent — it was already welded to the barrel. First-person rounds spawn on the boresight 0.7 m from the eye, and at 550 m/s on a 30 Hz sim a round's first rendered frame is already 18–55 m downrange — past the streak cap. Every tracer popped in mid-field with a dark gap back to the flash.
  • Player rounds carry the true barrel-tip (Bullet.muzzle, render-only — ballistics stay on the boresight, so the crosshair remains exactly true).
  • A launch bridge stretches the streak back to the muzzle for the first 30 m, so the tracer visibly grows out of the flash before detaching into a normal trail.
  • AI and third-person tracers untouched; flash-light-pool and night-lighting invariants preserved.
Verified day & night in the FPS Lab on rifle, Lewis (right-offset barrel welds correctly) and Vickers — no gap, no pop-in.

The trench overhaul

#17 · PR #21
Junction pits · duckboards · floating men · revetment
Root cause, measured live: communication trenches carved after the front line snapshotted the already-lowered floor, so every junction cut a second full depth — a measured −1.93 m pit against a −0.47 m floor. And men were synthetically hoisted 1.55 m so muzzles cleared the parapet — feet hovering on nothing.
  • Junctions: all trench lines now carve against one shared pristine snapshot — a clean union at one depth. Junction floor now −0.14 m; the pit is gone.
  • Fire step: a real bench carved into the enemy wall; fighting positions stand on it, feet planted (float gap 1.55 m → 0.12 m avg) while still sighting over the parapet. One shared constant drives both the carve and the sim formula, so cover/LOS tuning is numerically identical.
  • Duckboards: proper slatted boards — stringers, transverse treads, gaps, warp, snapped slats, mud — conforming to the floor.
  • Revetment: sandbag parapet course + timber plank walls; ~112k instanced triangles for only 2 extra draw calls.
Numeric heightfield probes + gallery captures before/after; craters still deform trench ground; rebased over all five sibling merges with no conflicts.

Coordinated infantry tactics

#12 · PR #16
Enemy AI & the adaptive director
  • Bounding overwatch: each squad splits into two leapfrog elements — one suppresses from cover while the other sprints crater-to-crater at +40% speed, swapping every ~3.4 s. One cheap squad-level tick; no per-soldier pathfinding.
  • NCOs: every squad gets a leader men rally toward; his death deals a section-wide morale blow and hesitation spike before a replacement steps up. Leader-sniping is real counterplay.
  • Legible director: purchased counters now head the intel report in plain words — “They have brought up snipers and storm parties to silence your machine guns.”
Deterministic headless probes: 3 leapfrog swaps with perfect element complementarity across 360 ticks, byte-identical across runs (all randomness seeded).

Veterancy — the named men grow

#13 · PR #19
Progression · deeds · despatches
  • XP from kills, waves survived and six detected deeds (coolness under fire, stretcher rescues, mending the line, bayonet charges, marksmanship, holding a post alone) promotes Pte → L/Cpl → Cpl → Sjt.
  • Modest perks — accuracy, rate of fire, suppression resistance, faster rally; Corporals and up radiate a small calm aura. Attachment, not superheroes.
  • Veterancy lives on the position: a lone rifleman's death takes it to the grave; a gun team's No. 2 inherits the gun.
  • Brass chevrons over veteran positions; deeds cited in letters home and honoured apart on the Roll of Honour — “✠ Mentioned in despatches.”
16/16 logic assertions; live promotion & despatch toasts; exact save/load round-trip of three veterans' ranks, deeds and service; old saves still load.

HUD & command UX

#10 · PR #18
Including the build-bar overflow bug
The reported bug: at 1280 px the 18 placeable cards ran out to ~1705 px — six cards unreachable, no scroll. Fixed by restacking the bottom HUD and splitting the bar into two labelled wrapping groups: The Company and Field Works. Every card reachable at every tested width.
  • Period-styled tooltips for every unit, defence and order (role, reach, crew, cost, cooldown, hotkey).
  • “Can't afford” now reads differently from “wrong phase”; requisition purse chip; wave-start hotkey shown.
  • Unit panel gains crew count, Nerve and ammo bars, live state pills; the LINE meter shifts amber→red as it drains; the wind vane flags gas blow-back home.
Verified at 1280×800 and 1024×720, no console errors, reduced-motion honoured.

Procedural audio overhaul

#11 · PR #15
Still zero samples — everything synthesized
  • Every small arm layers transient + body + trench slapback with per-shot jitter; the SMLE gets an audible bolt cycle, the Webley a cylinder click, the 18-pounder a twin-sub slam with carriage rattle.
  • The Vickers finally sounds distinct from the Lewis — heavier, darker, with a water-jacket clank.
  • A true supersonic crack (ballistic N-wave) for near misses, placed at closest approach.
  • Shell whistles grow a sub-octave partial; bursts gain ground-shaking low end; ambience beds crossfade continuously through dusk and dawn, with sparse birdsong when the front is quiet.
  • Brick-wall master limiter — worst-case stacked volleys peak at 0.94, under clipping.
Analyser tap on the master bus confirmed every layer triggers; zero errors through a 40-voice volley and a full day→night→day crossfade.
